About Me

Hi there!
I'm a front-end developer with a BSc in Psychology. I work with JavaScript, React, and other frameworks to build applications that are not only functional and scalable, but also intuitive.

My journey from psychology to front-end development was driven by my curiosity about people and how they interact with the world. Psychology taught me to understand human behaviour, while front-end development lets me apply that perspective to building engaging digital experiences. For me, it's about understanding people and creating what meets their needs.

My life beyond coding is full of discoveries. I spend my freetime reading books, watching movies and trying new food. Just as in my professional life, I value learning, intergrity and having new experiences.
